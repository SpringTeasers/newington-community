Things To Do in Newington

Parks, trails, skating, the library and the town's seasonal events, all within a 13.1-square-mile Hartford County town.

Getting started

Newington's recreation is mostly outdoors and mostly free. Mill Pond Park and Mill Pond Falls sit at the center of town, the Newington Greenway trail system takes in the Cedar Mountain hiking trail, and the Lucy Robbins Welles Library on Garfield Street runs programs for all ages. The town's biggest annual gathering is the Waterfall Festival each fall.

Annual & Recurring Events

  • Fall

    Waterfall Festival at Mill Pond Falls

    The town's fall festival at Mill Pond Falls, with local vendors, food and entertainment. Held annually each fall.

  • Summer

    Independence Day festivities

    Newington marks Independence Day with fireworks and community gatherings each July.

  • Summer

    Food Truck Fridays at Mill Pond Park

    Local food vendors gather at Mill Pond Park on Fridays during the season for community evenings.

  • Weekly in season

    Newington Farmers Market

    A seasonal weekly market with local produce and goods.

  • Recurring

    Library programs

    The Lucy Robbins Welles Library runs story times, reading groups and other programs throughout the year; see the library page for the current schedule.

  • Recurring

    Town Council and board meetings

    Regularly scheduled public meetings. Dates and agendas are posted on the town calendar.

Dates, times and venues change every year — confirm with the organiser or on the official town calendar: newingtonct.gov/calendar.aspx (opens in a new tab)

Family & Seasonal

  • Ice skating at Newington Ice Arena

    Public skate sessions, lessons and youth hockey make the arena a year-round option for families.

  • Walking and picnicking at Mill Pond Park

    The town center park, with Mill Pond Falls, paths and picnic areas.

  • Library programs for children

    Story times and children's programming at the Lucy Robbins Welles Library, 100 Garfield Street.

  • Youth sports and leagues

    Town fields and recreation programs support youth and adult leagues; see the recreation department for registration.

  • Fall foliage on the Cedar Mountain trail

    The Cedar Mountain hiking trail, off Willard Avenue, is part of the Newington Greenway system.
