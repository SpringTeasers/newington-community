Welcome to Newington

A community guide to Newington, Connecticut — a Hartford County town of about 30,536 people, eight miles south of Hartford, with a town green, a public library on Garfield Street, and one of the busiest retail corridors in central Connecticut.

About this guide

Newington was incorporated in 1871 and covers 13.1 square miles in Hartford County, Connecticut, in the Capitol Planning Region. It sits eight miles south of Hartford, and the Berlin Turnpike (State Route 15) runs through town as its main commercial corridor.

This site gathers the practical parts of the town in one place — what to do, where to shop and eat, how to reach town offices, and what is happening — with links out to the official sources for anything that can change.
