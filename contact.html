Contact

How to reach this site, how to correct a listing, and the town numbers you are most likely to need.

Contact this site

This site is maintained independently as a community guide to Newington, Connecticut. If something here is wrong, out of date or missing, tell us what you found — the entry, the detail that is wrong, and the correct detail with where you saw it. A source or a link helps us verify it faster.

This site does not currently have an email address of its own; it is maintained independently and has no mailbox to publish yet. So there is no mailto: link on this page. Use the guidance in the correction block below, and the town routes listed under Key town contacts, to reach the right office.

We read feedback that reaches us through the town routes listed below.

You can also browse the local business directory or the town services and links page before you write.

Submit a listing correction

Use the directory to check the current entry first, then send a correction through the channels below. Corrections are checked against the business's or the town's own source before anything changes.

What to send

  1. The business or town-office name as it appears on this site.
  2. What is wrong — the address, the phone number, the website, the hours, or a closed business.
  3. The correct detail, and where you saw it (the business's own website, a town page, or a posted notice).

This site has no mailbox of its own yet. Until one is set up, corrections can be sent through the channels listed below under Key town contacts, or raised with the relevant business or office directly. No mailto: link is rendered here.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official Town of Newington website?

No. This is an independent community guide. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with the Town of Newington. The official town site is newingtonct.gov (opens in a new tab), and the footer of every page here says so.

Something on this site is wrong. What do I do?

Use Report a listing correction in the footer, or the correction block on this page. Tell us the entry, what is wrong, and the correct detail with where you saw it. We check it against the business's or the town's own source before changing anything.

Can you answer a question about my taxes, my trash collection or a permit?

No. Those are town matters, and only the town can answer them accurately. Start with Town Hall on 860-665-8500 or the town's departments index, https://www.newingtonct.gov/101/Departments (opens in a new tab).

How current is the information here?

The directory, services list and events are reviewed periodically. Hours, phone numbers and dates change — especially for businesses and for seasonal events. Confirm the detail with the business or the office directly before you rely on it.

Do you have a mailing list or take advertising?

No. This site carries no mailing list, no advertising and no sponsorship, and it does not sell anything.

Disclaimer

  • This site is an independent community resource. It is not an official Town of Newington website and is not affiliated with the town.
  • Town details published here are drawn from official town pages and verified sources, and each page links to the official source for anything that can change.
  • For official town matters — records, permits, taxes, schools, emergencies — contact the town or the relevant district directly. Call 911 in an emergency.
