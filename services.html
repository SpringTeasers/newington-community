Licenses, Permits & Records

Where to start

Most permits, licenses and records requests are handled by the responsible town department. Start at the departments index, or call the Town Hall main line and ask for the office you need.

  • Town Hall main line: 860-665-8500
  • Building, land use and zoning: handled by the town's land-use offices — use the departments index and ask for building, planning and zoning, or the wetlands and inland wetlands office.
  • Vital records and town clerk: the Town Clerk's office handles birth, marriage and death records and town records — use the departments index and ask for the Town Clerk.

Permit fees, forms and office hours change. Confirm with the department before you visit.
