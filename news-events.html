Events through the year

Newington's larger events recur through the year, and most are announced with dates only a few weeks ahead. The items below are recurring or typical events, labelled by season, not fixed 2026 dates. For confirmed dates and times, use the official town calendar.

  • Fall

    Waterfall Festival at Mill Pond Falls

    Mill Pond Falls, town center, Newington

    The town's fall festival at Mill Pond Falls, with local vendors, food and community events.

    Official town calendar → (opens in a new tab)

  • Summer

    Independence Day festivities

    Newington

    Newington marks Independence Day with fireworks and community gatherings each July.

  • Summer

    Food Truck Fridays at Mill Pond Park

    Mill Pond Park, Newington

    Local food vendors gather at Mill Pond Park on Fridays during the season.

  • Weekly in season

    Newington Farmers Market

    Newington

    A seasonal weekly market with local produce and goods.

  • Recurring

    Library programs at the Lucy Robbins Welles Library

    Lucy Robbins Welles Library, 100 Garfield Street, Newington, CT 06111

    Story times, reading groups and community programs throughout the year.

    Library page → (opens in a new tab)

  • Recurring

    Town Council and town board meetings

    Newington Town Hall, 200 Garfield Street, Newington, CT 06111

    Regularly scheduled public meetings; agendas and minutes are posted by the town.

    Town calendar → (opens in a new tab)

Local news

This site does not carry a news feed. Newington news that matters to residents — meeting agendas, road and collection changes, budget notices and school announcements — is published by the town and by the local press. These are the standing sources.

  • Standing

    Town meeting agendas and minutes

    Agenda packets, minutes and public notices for the Town Council and town boards are posted by the town ahead of each meeting. This is the first place to look for a decision that affects your street or your taxes.

    Town calendar → (opens in a new tab)

  • Standing

    Town Hall hours and office closures

    Town Hall is open Monday, Wednesday and Thursday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; Tuesday 8:30 a.m.–6:00 p.m.; Friday 8:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m. Holiday closures are posted by the town.

    Town of Newington → (opens in a new tab)

  • Standing

    Newington Public Schools announcements

    Calendar changes, delays and district announcements are published by the district, not by this site.

    Newington Public Schools → (opens in a new tab)

  • Standing

    Library news and programs

    The Lucy Robbins Welles Library publishes its own program schedule and service updates.

    Lucy Robbins Welles Library → (opens in a new tab)

  • Standing

    Connecticut Department of Transportation projects

    The Connecticut DOT is headquartered in Newington, and state road and construction notices that affect the Berlin Turnpike and other state routes are published by the state.

    Town of Newington → (opens in a new tab) — the town re-posts state notices that affect local roads.
